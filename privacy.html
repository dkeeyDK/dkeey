Privacy

Hidden amounts. Keys that stay with you. And a research idea: a balance whose look keeps changing.

Plan and research, not live

Nothing here is deployed or audited. The demo below is a teaching toy that runs in your browser. It is the maths only: it is not the DK token and nothing is on a chain.

What DK plans

  • Hidden amounts. DK is planned as a Solana token with confidential balances, so holding and sending amounts are encrypted.
  • Keys stay with you. They come from your wallet signature. DKEEY keeps no master key that can read your balance.
  • You choose who sees. If you ever want to show your balance to someone, you can. We cannot do it for you.
  • Honest limits. Addresses stay visible. Entering or leaving the pool shows an amount.

What this hides, and what it does not

  • Hides: when a balance really changes. A refresh and a real payment look the same from outside.
  • Does not hide: addresses, or transfers that were already seen.
  • Not proven yet: that this can run on a chain. Spending after a refresh needs proofs, and that is the open research step.

Research and design draft. Not audited, not deployed. We do not call it quantum-proof or unbreakable. Nothing here is an offer or advice. You can lose everything you put into any crypto asset.
