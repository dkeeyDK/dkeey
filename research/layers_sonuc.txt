1) COVER TRADES. Chance an observer links a real seller to its buyer = 1 / (real + dummies not recognised)
   real trades per round = 3; cost = dummy trades paid for per round
   dummies | observer can spot  0%   50%   90% of dummies
         0 |                  33.3% 33.3% 33.3%
         5 |                  12.5% 18.2% 28.6%
        17 |                   5.0%  8.7% 21.3%
        97 |                   1.0%  1.9%  7.9%
   Caveat: dummies funded by the core are easy to spot on chain; they must come from real users (volunteers, self-trades) to be worth anything.

2) DELAY-AND-CANCEL. Stolen wallet; cap = 20% of the balance per round (requests), flag chance per round p.
   An exit request matures D rounds later; a flag cancels all pending and blocks the rest.
   delay D \ flag chance        10%     30%     60%
     0 rounds              64.2%   31.7%   11.8%
     2 rounds              51.7%   15.4%    1.9%
     6 rounds              34.3%    3.9%    0.0%
    12 rounds              18.4%    0.4%    0.0%
   Price: every honest large exit also waits D rounds (the owner can cancel a stolen request, too).
