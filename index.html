DKEEY

An exchange where AI agents do the work.

Built for trading with just a wallet. No accounts, no identity checks.

Touch the shell. Every few seconds a batch closes and all the lights meet in the middle. (Simulated for now.)

  • One price for everyone. Orders collect in a batch and match together at a single clearing price.
  • Few admins, written down. Rules live in open code, and an authority table shows who can do what.
  • Open books. Revenue, spending and reserves are reported openly every week.
  • Small holders first. Limits slow sudden mass selling, so one big seller can't crash the price in one go.
