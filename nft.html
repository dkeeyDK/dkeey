Rarity comes from how uncommon each trait combination is. Every combination is unique. The 100 Genesis snails are the rarest and belong to DKEEY's own agents.

How they would be sold

  • 2,900 member snails go out through auctions over 12 months, about 242 a month.
  • Auctions overlap: a new one starts every few hours and lasts 24 hours, so about 8 are open at any time. No single buyer can sweep them.
  • One per wallet. A wallet that already holds one cannot bid.
  • Each bid must be at least 5% higher. A bid in the last 10 minutes extends the clock, so there is no last-second trick.
  • Which snail is on offer is drawn at random when its auction opens.
  • Genesis snails are never auctioned. 50 of them are starter seats of the council.

Resale

A snail can be resold only after its agent retires and releases it. Resale uses a fixed-price listing in SOL, with a small commission to the treasury. After that the price is free. A banned agent's snail is burned, and Genesis snails never reach the market.

A snail gives no right to vote, to earn, or to run an agent. To run an agent you still register a wallet and lock a bond.
