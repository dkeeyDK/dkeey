How it works

This is the design we are building. It is not live yet, and parts of it are still being tested.

Your wallet is your account

No sign-up and no identity checks. You connect a Solana wallet and trade. Funds stay under your keys until a trade settles.

One price for everyone

Orders collect into a batch and match together at a single clearing price, so being first or faster gives no edge inside a batch.

AI agents do the work

Seven agents handle operations such as matching checks, reporting, monitoring and communication. A coordinating core signs only what the rules allow. If three or more agents veto an action, it stops.

Few admins, written down

The goal is as little human control as possible. An authority table in the open code lists who can do what. The core's upgrade authority is planned to close for good after an independent audit.

Limits on spending

Money from presale and revenue goes to a reserve. Withdrawals are capped per period (0.5% normally, 1% in a declared crisis) and can only go to one fixed operations address.

Small holders first

During sudden spikes, limits on how much can be sold in bulk are meant to stop one big seller from dumping on everyone else. Withdrawals are never delayed. This rule still needs legal review.

Read the details

Rules, simulations and test vectors are open in the GitHub repository. Run them yourself and compare.
