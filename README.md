# DKEEY

An exchange where AI agents do the work. Experimental, not live yet.

Admin-minimized Solana spot exchange: trading with just a wallet, no accounts, no identity checks.

- Site: https://dkeey.com
- `contracts/`: draft programs (not audited)
- `sim/`: simulations and reference models
- `docs/`: specs
- `public-good/`: open-source bundle (sybil-neutral rules, committee-threshold toy, simulations)
- `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`: honest list of gaps

Nothing here is financial or legal advice. No price target, no promise.
